Terms & Conditions.

These terms cover booking and attending an appointment at any LaVie Nail Lounge studio, and using this website. Booking an appointment means accepting them.

Last updated 29 July 2026 · Applies to lavienaillounge.ca and to all three studios

1. Who these terms are with

“LaVie”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean LaVie Nail Lounge, which operates three studios in British Columbia:

  • Fleetwood — 15988 Fraser Hwy Unit #202, Surrey, BC V4N 0X8 · 778‑565‑3515
  • Newton — 7218 King George Blvd #118, Surrey, BC V3W 5A5 · 778‑592‑1049
  • Langley — 8850 Walnut Grove Dr, Langley Twp, BC V1M 2C9 · 604‑888‑1619 (previously trading as 3D Nails Spa)

Each studio has its own team, its own price menu and its own booking calendar. Where these terms refer to “the studio”, they mean the location you booked with.

2. Booking an appointment

Appointments are booked online through Dash Booking, or by telephoning a studio directly. Online bookings are:

  • Confirmed immediately. There is no waiting period for a studio to accept a booking.
  • Available up to 60 days ahead. The calendar does not open beyond that.
  • Free to place. No deposit or card detail is required to hold an online appointment.

There is no minimum notice period built into the booking system, so a same-day slot can be taken if one is free. Availability shown in the calendar is live, but a slot is only yours once the booking is confirmed.

3. Prices and durations

Every service is published with a price and an expected duration, both on this website and again in the booking system at the point you select it. Prices are in Canadian dollars.

  • Menus differ by studio. Fleetwood, Newton and Langley do not carry identical service lists or identical prices. The menu on each studio's page is that studio's own.
  • “From” prices are starting prices. Where a line reads “From $X”, the final amount depends on the work — most often nail length, the condition of the existing set, or the complexity of a design. Your technician will tell you the price before starting.
  • Add-ons are priced separately. Longer nails, design work, shellac and removals are their own line items rather than silent additions to the bill.
  • Prices may change. The booking system is the authority at the time you book. If a price on this website disagrees with the booking system, the booking system governs, and we would be grateful if you told us so we can correct the page.

Applicable taxes are added where required. Payment is taken in studio at the end of your appointment unless we have agreed otherwise with you.

4. Changing or cancelling

Plans change — please tell us as early as you can so the slot can be offered to someone else. You can change or cancel through the confirmation you received from the booking system, or by telephoning the studio.

No deposit is taken for an online booking, so cancelling costs you nothing. Any cancellation, late-arrival or no-show terms that apply to a particular booking are the ones displayed in the booking system when you place it.

If you arrive late we will do as much of the booked service as the remaining time allows, or offer to rebook. This protects the appointment of the client after you.

5. Repairs

Repairs to work done at LaVie are booked as their own short appointment. What you pay depends on how long ago the set was applied — our booking system distinguishes a repair within the policy period from one after seven days. Please confirm the applicable rate with the studio when you book the repair.

Repairs cover the work we did. Damage caused by wear, impact, chemicals or work carried out elsewhere is charged as a new service.

6. Gift cards

Gift cards can be bought online through any studio's booking page, or in studio. They are redeemable against services, are not exchangeable for cash, and cannot be replaced if lost. Please check with the studio before assuming a card bought at one location can be redeemed at another — the three studios run separate booking accounts.

7. Your appointment

So that we can work safely, please tell your technician before we begin about any allergy, skin or nail condition, infection, recent injury, pregnancy, or medication that affects your skin or healing. We may decline or modify a service where we believe it is not safe to proceed — for you, for our team, or for other clients. That judgement rests with the studio.

Waxing and lash services are not suitable in every circumstance, and we may ask you to patch-test or to seek medical advice first.

Children are welcome for the children's services on the menu. Anyone under the age of majority should be accompanied by a parent or guardian, who is responsible for consenting to the service.

We are not able to take responsibility for personal belongings left in the studio.

8. Photographs of our work

We photograph finished sets for our portfolio and our Instagram accounts. Tell your technician — before or after — if you would prefer your hands or your set were not photographed or posted, and we will not use them. If something of yours is already published and you want it taken down, contact the studio and we will remove it.

9. If something is not right

Tell us. Contact the studio that did the work as soon as you notice a problem, and give us the chance to put it right — that is nearly always quicker and better than anything else. This does not affect your rights under the Business Practices and Consumer Protection Act or other applicable British Columbia law.
