Note for the studio — two items to confirm

Fleetwood's booking system lists two repair line items: Fix (under Policy <7 Days) at $7 and Fix (>7 Days) at $0. As written, the later repair is the free one, which reads as the reverse of the usual arrangement. The labels above follow the booking system exactly — please confirm which way round it should be, and both this page and the FAQ will be corrected.

Fleetwood's add-ons group also carries a second Design entry at $0 for 15 minutes. It has been left off this page as a probable duplicate — confirm whether it is a distinct complimentary option that should be shown.
