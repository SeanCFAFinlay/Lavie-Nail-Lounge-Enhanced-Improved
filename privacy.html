Privacy Policy.

LaVie Nail Lounge collects a small amount of personal information — essentially what is needed to hold an appointment and to reach you about it. This page sets out exactly what, why, who else sees it, and how to get it back or have it deleted.

Last updated 29 July 2026 · Governed by BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA)

1. Who is responsible

LaVie Nail Lounge is the organisation responsible for personal information collected through this website and in our studios. We operate three locations in British Columbia:

  • Fleetwood — 15988 Fraser Hwy Unit #202, Surrey, BC V4N 0X8 · 778‑565‑3515
  • Newton — 7218 King George Blvd #118, Surrey, BC V3W 5A5 · 778‑592‑1049
  • Langley — 8850 Walnut Grove Dr, Langley Twp, BC V1M 2C9 · 604‑888‑1619

We are a private-sector organisation in British Columbia, so the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) applies to us. Our privacy contact is the studio manager at the Fleetwood address above.

2. What we collect

When you book an appointment

Booking is handled by Dash Booking, a third-party salon booking platform. To hold an appointment it collects:

  • your name;
  • your mobile number, so we can send the confirmation and reach you if something changes;
  • your email address, where you give one;
  • the service, studio, technician and time you selected;
  • your appointment history with us, and any note a technician adds about your preferences;
  • your date of birth, only if you choose to give it for a birthday offer.

We do not ask for or store card numbers on this website. No deposit or card detail is needed to book online. Payment is taken in studio, and card payments are processed by our payment terminal provider, which handles the card data — we see only the result of the transaction.

When you tell us about your health

Before some services we ask about allergies, skin or nail conditions, infections, recent injuries, pregnancy or medication, because they affect whether a treatment is safe. This is sensitive information. We use it only to decide how to carry out your service safely, we tell only the technicians who need to know, and we do not use it for marketing.

When you simply visit this website

You can read every page — prices, hours, technicians, the gallery — without giving us anything. We do not run a newsletter sign-up or a contact form. What is collected is described under “Analytics” below.

When you call us

We take the details needed to make the booking. We do not record calls.

3. Why we use it, and your consent

We use personal information to book and deliver your appointment, to contact you about it, to take payment, to keep the records a business is required to keep, and to carry out a service safely. Under PIPA we rely on your consent, which you give by making a booking; for some records — such as those we must keep for tax purposes — we rely on the legal obligation instead.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing. If we ever want to send you promotional messages we will ask separately, and every such message will carry a way to stop them.

4. Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers. Each of them handles information on our behalf, under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Dash Booking — the booking calendar embedded on this site and linked from it. It holds your booking record and sends your confirmation and reminders. When you use the embedded calendar you are interacting with Dash Booking directly.
  • Google Maps — the maps on our location pages are embedded from Google. When a map loads, Google receives your IP address and may set cookies. If you would rather that did not happen, use the “Directions” buttons instead, which do nothing until you click them.
  • Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager, and Microsoft Clarity — website analytics, described in the next section.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and keeps standard server logs.
  • Our payment terminal provider, for in-studio card payments.

Some of these providers operate outside Canada, mainly in the United States, so information may be stored on or accessed from servers in another country and be subject to the laws there. We keep the amount of information involved to the minimum needed.

5. Analytics and cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. Our analytics providers may set cookies or use similar storage to recognise a returning browser:

  • Google Analytics 4, via Google Tag Manager — counts visits, which pages are read and which booking buttons are used, so we know what is worth improving. IP addresses are anonymised.
  • Microsoft Clarity — records anonymised page interactions such as scrolling and clicks, to show where a page is confusing. Clarity masks text content by default.

We use this only in aggregate. We do not try to identify individual visitors from it, and we do not connect it to your booking record.

Turning it off. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal, this site loads no analytics at all — no tag is requested and no cookie is set. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies for a site, and both Google and Microsoft publish their own opt-outs.

6. Photographs

We photograph finished sets for our portfolio and Instagram. These show hands, nails or lashes, not faces, and they are not captioned with your name. Tell your technician if you would rather your work were not photographed or posted, and we will not use it. If something is already published and you want it removed, contact the studio and we will take it down.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your booking record for as long as you remain a client and for a reasonable period afterwards, so that a returning client's history and preferences are still there. Financial records are kept for the period Canadian tax law requires. Health notes are kept only while they are relevant to your treatment. When information is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for, or for a legal requirement, we delete it or make it anonymous.

You can ask us to delete your record sooner — see below.

8. Keeping it secure

Booking records sit in Dash Booking behind an account login held by studio staff. Access is limited to the people who need it to run the studio. This website is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever created a real risk of significant harm to you, we would notify you and the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

9. Your rights

Under PIPA you may:

  • Ask what we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
  • Ask us to correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Withdraw your consent to our use of it, with reasonable notice — though we may then be unable to hold appointments for you;
  • Ask us to delete what we are not required to keep;
  • Complain if you think we have got it wrong.

Call any studio or write to the address in section 11. We will respond within 30 business days, as PIPA requires, or tell you if we need an extension and why. There is no charge for a routine request. We may need to confirm your identity first, so that we do not hand your information to someone else.

10. If you are not satisfied

Please raise it with us first — most things are a misunderstanding and are fixed quickly. If you are still not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, at oipc.bc.ca.
